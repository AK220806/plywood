Our Craft

We peel, we dry, we press — slowly.

Each log is hand-selected from FSC-certified plantations, peeled into continuous veneers, then kiln-dried to a moisture content of 8±2%. The plies are layered cross-grain, glued with melamine-fortified resins, and pressed at 140°C under 18 kg/cm².

The result? Panels that don't bow, delaminate or warp — even after decades of tropical humidity. We test every batch in our in-house lab before it ever leaves the yard.

From Log to Living Room

Four stages. No shortcuts.

01

Select & Peel

FSC hardwood logs are debarked, soaked, then rotary-peeled into continuous veneer leaves 1.2mm thin.

02

Kiln Dry

Veneers are conditioned to 8% moisture in temperature-controlled kilns — preventing every future warp.

03

Layer & Glue

Cross-grain assembly with phenol-formaldehyde resin — bonds stronger than the wood itself.

04

Press & Calibrate

Hot-pressed under 18kg/cm², then sanded to a uniform ±0.2mm tolerance. Ready for your craft.
